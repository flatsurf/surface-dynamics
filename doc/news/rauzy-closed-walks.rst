**Added:**

* Added ``iet.rauzy_closed_walks`` to iterate over the primitive complete
  closed walks of the reduced Rauzy diagram of an Abelian permutation or of a
  linear involution (generalized permutation), labelled or reduced, or of a
  component of an Abelian or of a quadratic stratum, as closed flip sequences.
  The walks keep the alphabet of the permutation. The search is
  also available as the method ``closed_walks`` of
  ``LazyReducedRauzyDiagram``.

* Added ``weighted_necklaces`` in ``surface_dynamics.misc.necklaces`` to
  iterate over the necklaces or Lyndon words of bounded weight over an
  alphabet of weighted letters, optionally restricted to the words whose
  letters cover a set of bits and with the product of the letters in a
  monoid.
