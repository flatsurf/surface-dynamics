**Added:**

* Added ``iet.rauzy_closed_walks`` to iterate over the primitive complete
  closed walks of the reduced Rauzy diagram of an Abelian permutation, labelled
  or reduced, or of a component of an Abelian stratum, as closed flip
  sequences. The walks keep the alphabet of the permutation. The search is
  also available as the method ``closed_walks`` of
  ``LazyReducedRauzyDiagram``.
